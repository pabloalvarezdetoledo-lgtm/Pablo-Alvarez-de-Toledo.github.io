## About this entry

This project is an independent, academic-style equity research exercise on NVIDIA Corporation, the US-listed designer of graphics processors and accelerated-computing systems. It sets out the strongest form of the bullish consensus case — demand verified by hyperscaler capital-expenditure filings, a widening customer base and supply-constrained growth guidance — before testing it against the working-capital burden of that growth, the multiple's dependence on a still-unrealised near-term growth assumption, and the increasingly circular financing relationships between NVIDIA and some of its largest customers.

The analysis triangulates a twelve-month target price from a discounted free-cash-flow-to-firm model and forward P/E and EV/EBIT multiples benchmarked against a peer group of listed semiconductor and foundry companies, all anchored to the same forecast fiscal year to avoid mixing valuation horizons. A qualitative capital-cycle section, again drawn from the Austrian tradition of capital theory, is used solely to justify weighting the downside scenario above what a simple extrapolation of analyst estimates would suggest.

Like the other case studies in this portfolio, this is an independent academic project completed outside any employer relationship. It is not an official research product of any institution, and the target price and hold-type recommendation it derives describe the output of the exercise's methodology rather than investment advice offered to readers.

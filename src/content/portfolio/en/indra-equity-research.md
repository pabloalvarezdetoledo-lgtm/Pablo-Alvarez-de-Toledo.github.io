## About this entry

This project is an **independent, academic-style equity research exercise** applying valuation and financial-statement analysis methods to Indra Group, S.A. (BME: IDR), the Spanish aerospace, defense, space, air-traffic-management and IT-services group. It is not an official research product of any employer, and it draws exclusively on public sources — regulatory filings, results releases, sector-multiple trackers and market data.

The report works through Indra's five reporting divisions, reconciles the reported order book and cash-flow figures against the portion attributable to client-financed advances, and builds a cost of capital from a euro risk-free rate, a market risk premium and a revenue-weighted country risk premium. It derives a twelve-month target price as the simple average of a sum-of-the-parts valuation, a discounted free-cash-flow model and a price/earnings cross-check, and complements the quantitative model with bull/base/bear scenarios and a qualitative capital-cycle framework used explicitly to stress-test — never to alter — the underlying assumptions.

As with the other case studies on this site, this entry is a personal academic exercise: it applies a research process, not a live investment service, and any conclusions reflect a point-in-time analytical judgement rather than ongoing coverage.

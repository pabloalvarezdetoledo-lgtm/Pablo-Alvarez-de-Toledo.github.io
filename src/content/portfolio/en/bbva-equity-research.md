## About this entry

This project is an **independent, academic-style equity research report** on Banco Bilbao Vizcaya Argentaria (BBVA), applying institutional-style equity research methods to a real, listed European banking group. It is not an official research product of any employer, and it does not reproduce confidential, proprietary or internal information from any organisation Pablo has worked with.

The report profiles BBVA's business mix and geographic earnings composition — in particular the contrast between its highly profitable but more volatile Mexican franchise and its lower-risk Spanish business — reviews the macroeconomic and competitive backdrop for European and Latin American banking, and analyses recent financial performance, credit quality and capital adequacy. Because banks are not conventionally valued through a discounted free-cash-flow-to-firm framework, the valuation section instead combines a Gordon-growth-derived target multiple on tangible book value, a discounted distributable-cash-flow-to-shareholder approach, and trading comparables against listed Spanish peers, with the cost of equity built from CAPM and a profit-weighted country risk premium. The analysis concludes with sensitivity testing, scenario analysis, and a mapped set of risks to the thesis.

As with the other case studies on this site, this is an academic exercise rather than live financial advice: the report should be read as a demonstration of research and valuation method, not as a personalised investment recommendation, and any figures or conclusions it reaches reflect estimates as of the stated valuation date rather than an ongoing, updated view.

## About this entry

This project is an **independent, academic-style equity research report** on Repsol, applying institutional-style equity research methods to a real, listed, integrated energy company. It is not an official research product of any employer.

The report examines Repsol's integrated business model across Upstream, Industrial, Client and Low-Carbon Generation, reviews capital allocation, balance-sheet discipline and corporate governance, and situates the company within the broader sector backdrop for global oil and gas supply, demand and refining margins. Because a large share of the group's 2026 earnings step-up reflects an unusually strong, and likely cyclical, refining margin environment together with favourable inventory effects, the analysis explicitly normalises EBITDA (on a current-cost-of-supply basis) and free cash flow before valuing the business, combining a discounted-cash-flow-to-the-firm model with trading comparables against listed European integrated energy peers, complemented by sensitivity and scenario analysis and a qualitative assessment of capital-cycle risk.

As with the other case studies on this site, this is an academic exercise rather than live financial advice: the report should be read as a demonstration of research and valuation method, not as a personalised investment recommendation, and any figures or conclusions it reaches reflect estimates as of the stated valuation date rather than an ongoing, updated view.

## About this entry

This project is an **independent, academic-style equity research exercise** applying valuation and financial-statement analysis methods to Meta Platforms, Inc. (NASDAQ: META), the technology and social-media company behind Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and Threads. It is not an official research product of any employer, and it draws exclusively on public sources — SEC filings, results releases, analyst consensus data and market pricing.

The report separates Meta's advertising-driven Family of Apps segment from the loss-making Reality Labs segment, and situates both against a step-change in capital expenditure directed at AI infrastructure. It derives a twelve-month target price as a weighted average of a discounted free-cash-flow model, a price/earnings multiple and an EV/EBITDA multiple, cross-checked against a comparable set of large technology and cloud-infrastructure peers, and complements the quantitative model with scenario and sensitivity analysis and a qualitative capital-cycle framework applied to the broader AI infrastructure buildout across the sector.

As with the other case studies on this site, this entry is a personal academic exercise: it applies a research process, not a live investment service, and any conclusions reflect a point-in-time analytical judgement rather than ongoing coverage.

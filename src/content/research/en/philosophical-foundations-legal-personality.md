## About this entry

This working paper is forthcoming. It belongs to the Legal Philosophy strand of the research programme, and asks a question that is prior to most doctrinal discussions of rights: whether legal personality is something the legal order *grants* to human beings, or whether it instead *recognises* a status that the human being holds as an original matter, independent of any grant.

**Status: forthcoming.** Only the research question and a brief note on method are published here. No abstract or findings are shown, because the paper has not yet been written in a form ready to share — publishing draft conclusions in advance would misrepresent the state of the work.
